Formulas and standards reference

Copper resistance at conductor temperature θ: ρθ = (1/58) × [1 + 0.00393(θ−20)] Ω·mm²/m. For DC and 1-phase AC, ΔV ≈ I × 2L × ρθ/(S×n). For 3-phase AC, ΔV ≈ √3 × I × L × ρθ/(S×n), with cable reactance ignored.

Ampacity references: ISO 13297:2020 Annex A Tables A.1/A.2 (metric stranded copper 70/90/105°C and engine-space/bundle correction), via EN ISO 13297:2021. ISO 13297 covers ordinary DC systems up to 50 V nominal and single-phase AC up to 250 V. Electric propulsion and three-phase installations are covered by other standards. For the full installation, consult the published standard and cable manufacturer's datasheet.

Further details: ISO 13297:2020 · Blue Sea: choosing circuit protection · Victron Wiring Unlimited.
