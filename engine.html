Engineering limitations & sources

Review before specification
Calculation approach & official VETUS references

Displacement power screening: shared Indalo resistance model uses ITTC-1957 friction coefficient, a wetted-area approximation and a NON-STANDARD wave term. The estimate is low confidence until calibrated to a measured shaft-power trial. Hull shapes and sea state can change actual powering substantially. The energy conversion uses the user-entered overall shaft-to-effective-power efficiency.

Planing: empirical Crouch formula P(shaft hp) = loaded weight(lb) × [target speed(knots)/C]², C calibrated to comparable vessels. It should not be used for displacement hulls and is unreliable in the hump/transitional region. The default coefficient is 180, benchmarked against two published fast-cruiser examples; individual hulls may differ significantly. The target is treated as near-maximum speed.

Product ratings and listed families are sourced from official M-Line, H-Line, D-Line and E-Line listings (October 2026).
