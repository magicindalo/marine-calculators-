Where are you looking?

Enter a UK postcode or town/city. You can also let your phone find a nearby UK postcode.

How the dealer search works

1
Enter a postcode or choose Use My Location, then tap Find VETUS suppliers.
2
VETUS's official dealer finder opens. Paste or enter your postcode in its Postal code or city search box to find nearby suppliers.

Dealer locations and authorised dealer categories come from VETUS, not a stored list in this calculator. The external dealer finder needs an internet connection. You may also open the official VETUS locator directly.

Location and privacy

Your location is requested only if you tap Use My Location. If you allow access, your coordinates are sent to Postcodes.io to identify a nearby UK postcode. This app does not store your location or postcode. You will need to enter or paste the location into the VETUS website to finish the live dealer search.
