Calculation notes and references

Displacement hull speed = 1.34 × √(LWL in feet), in knots. The powering estimate uses the existing Indalo friction and simplified residual resistance model. Sea-trial measured total shaft power can calibrate the model.

Diesel ratings / E-Line product models are from the VETUS diesel engine catalogue and VETUS E-Line catalogue. Installation and torque/duty curves must be checked for each model.
