Method & sources

VETUS catalogue page 220 provides the wind calculation: pressure (N/m²) × lateral windage (m²) × 0.75 streamlining factor × wind-to-pivot arm (normally LOA/2), divided by thruster-to-pivot arm. Results in newtons are converted to kilogram-force with 9.80665 N/kgf. Beaufort 4–8 reference pressures are taken from the catalogue.

Page 221 supplies a reference chart of thruster ranges, tunnel diameters and approximate boat lengths. The in-app product list is supplemented by VETUS webshop information, including current BOW PRO Boosted models. All proposed models must be verified at order time; length guidance does not supersede the calculated force.
