How the calculation works

One series string contains the selected number of modules needed to reach the chosen 12/24/48 V system voltage. Parallel strings increase total Ah; total nominal energy is the sum of all battery energies. For LiFePO₄, a 12 V-class module uses 12.8 V nominal, a 24 V module uses 25.6 V and a 48 V module uses 51.2 V.

Charger rating uses the requested SOC increase, charging time, assumed charge efficiency and absorption taper allowance, checked against a preferred chemistry-specific charge C-rate. Charger output also allows for the entered loads operating during charging. It is constrained by a manufacturer current limit if supplied. Always use the battery manufacturer's charging and wiring instructions for final equipment selection.
