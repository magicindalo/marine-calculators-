Method & source information

The VETUS workbook “Vetus Calculator Book 12-10-2018.xls”, “Flow Back” sheet, contains the 25% backflow allowance and twice-backflow catalog capacity rule. These agree with the 2026–27 VETUS exhaust catalogue, page 115. Waterlock capacity and fittings are checked against catalogue pages 116–119 and current VETUS webshop ranges.

This calculator does not assess the entire exhaust system, including internal geometry, dynamic heeling, pump delivery during cranking, cooling water anti-siphon routing or actual backpressure. Approval must follow the engine manufacturer's limits and the relevant VETUS installation manual.
